4.Amino Acids
- Structure
    - Central to this structure is the tetrahedral 四面体 alpha carbon (Cα)
        - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/生物化学/images/790450_c842cd96-2fb3-4fbc-9826-4687df41cb3f.png)
    - Cα covalently bonded to
        - the amino group
        - the carboxyl group
        - a hydrogen
        - a variable side chain R
    - In neutral solution (pH 7), the carboxyl group exists as –COO- and the amino group as –NH3+.
    - Peptide Bonds
        - The amino and carboxyl groups of amino acids can react in a head-to-tail fashion, eliminating a water molecule and forming a covalent amide酰胺 linkage, a peptide bond
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/生物化学/images/790450_6bc0a66f-444e-4a89-a57b-7287027b1860.png)
        - The equilibrium for this reaction in aqueous solution favors peptide bond hydrolysis.

- electric properties
    - Amino Acids Are Weak Polyprotic Acids 多质子酸
        - The common amino acids are all weak polyprotic acids.
        - The ionizable 可电离的 groups are not strongly dissociating ones, and the degree of dissociation thus depends on the pH of the medium.
        - All the amino acids contain at least two dissociable hydrogens.
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/生物化学/images/790450_fee0b46f-2880-4902-aea5-b307a169519d.png)
    - Dissociation of Electrolytes 电解质
        - Ka ionization constant
            - states the extent to which a substance forms ions in water
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/生物化学/images/790450_03c85ff2-a20d-456d-d751-25e81973e661.png)
            - pKa =-log Ka
        - titration curve 滴定曲线
            以acetic acid醋酸举例
            - At the point where half of the HAc has been neutralized, that is, where 0.5 equivalent of OH has been added, the concentrations of HAc and Ac are equal and pH=pKa for HAc.
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/生物化学/images/790450_0f42b476-ab63-497b-c49b-affe94f97f97.png)
            - Thus, we have an experimental method for determining the pKa values of weak electrolytes.
            - These pKa values lie at the midpoint of their respective titration curves.
            - Note that the titration curve is relatively flat at pH values near the pKa, in other words, the pH changes relatively little as OH is added in this region of the titration curve.
            - 图
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/生物化学/images/790450_20fc90ed-3902-406d-b108-fb0832c6b5b8.png)
            - A biologically important point is revealed by the basic shape of the titration curves of weak electrolytes, in the region of the pKa, pH remains relatively unaffected as increments of OH- (or H+) are added. The weak acid and its conjugate base are acting as a buffer.
                弱电解质滴定曲线的基本形态揭示了一个重要的生物学现象, 在pKa附近区域，即使持续加入OH⁻（或H⁺），pH值仍能保持相对稳定，此时弱酸及其共轭碱正发挥着缓冲作用

- Optical Activity 旋光性 and Stereochemistry 立体化学
    - Chiral 手性
        - Except for glycine, all of the amino acids isolated from proteins have four different groups attached to the α-carbon atom.
        - In such a case, the α-carbon is said to be asymmetric or chiral
        - the two possible configurations for the α-carbon constitute nonsuperimposable mirror image isomers, or enantiomers 旋光对映体
            围绕α-碳原子的两种可能构型会形成无法完全重叠的镜像异构体，即旋光对映体
    - 旋光性
        - Enantiomeric molecules display a special property called optical activity—the ability to rotate the plane of polarization of plane-polarized light.
            旋光对映体分子显示一个特殊的属性，称为光学活性—可以旋转平面偏振光的偏振平面的能力。
        - Clockwise rotation of incident light is referred to as dextrorotatory behavior 右旋性 (+), and counterclockwise rotation is called levorotatory behavior 左旋的 (-).
            入射光顺时针旋转称为右旋性（+），逆时针旋转称为左旋性（-）
    - Nomenclature 命名 for Chiral Molecules
        - It is found that nearly all of the amino acids derived from natural proteins are of the L configuration.
            天然蛋白质来源的氨基酸几乎均为 L 型构型
        - Amino acids of the D configuration are nonetheless found in nature, especially as components of certain peptide antibiotics, such as valinomycin 缬氨霉素，gramicidin 短杆菌肽，and actinomycin D 放射菌素 D, and in the cell walls of certain microorganisms.
            D 型氨基酸在自然界中也存在，尤其作为某些肽类抗生素（如缬氨霉素、短杆菌肽、放射菌素 D）的组分，及某些微生物的细胞壁成分

- Separation and Analysis of Amino Acid Mixtures
    - 基于Mass
        - Centrifugation 离心法
    - 基于Electrical charge
        - Electrophoresis 电泳法
    - 基于Partition properties 分配性质
        the tendency to associate with one solvent or phase over another
        - Chromatography 层析法，色谱法
        - chromatography techniques depends on the repeated microscopic partitioning of a solute mixture between the available phases.
            层析技术基于溶质混合物在两相间的反复微观分配
        - Two phases—solid–liquid, liquid–liquid, or gas–liquid
            两相包括固 - 液、液 - 液或气 - 液
        - 流程
            - 1. 将氨基酸混合液加至阳离子交换柱
            - 2. 通过改变洗脱液条件，使不同氨基酸依次流出
            - 3. 分部收集流出液并检测，最终得到按时间分离的洗脱图谱
            - 图
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/生物化学/images/790450_41887eb0-5bb2-47c2-eda6-6e9ce39d3dff.png)
        - HPLC chromatogram of amino acids, x-保留时间, y-响应度, 信号越强, 代表该氨基酸的浓度越高
