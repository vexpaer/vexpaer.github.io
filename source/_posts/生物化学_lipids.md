8.lipids
- 基础
    - Lipids are a class of biological molecules defined by low solubility in water and high solubility in nonpolar solvents.
        脂质是一类生物分子，其定义为在水中溶解度低，而在非极性溶剂中溶解度高
    - As molecules that are largely hydrocarbon 烃 in nature, lipids represent highly reduced forms of carbon and,upon oxidation in metabolism, yield large amounts of energy.
        由于脂质本质上多为烃类分子，属于碳的高度还原形式，因此在代谢过程中发生氧化时，会产生大量能量
    - Lipids are thus the molecules of choice for metabolic energy storage.
    - The lipids found in biological systems are either hydrophobic 疏水(containingonly nonpolar groups) or amphipathic 两性分子的, which means they possess both polar and nonpolar groups.
    - The hydrophobic nature of lipid molecules allows membranes to act as effective barriers to more polar molecules.
- Fatty Acids
    - basic
        - A fatty acid is composed of a long hydrocarbon 烃 chain (tail) and a terminal carboxyl group 羧基 (head).
        - The carboxyl group is normally ionized under physiological conditions.
            羧基在生理条件下通常会发生电离
        - Fatty acids occur in large amounts in biological systems, but rarely in the free, uncomplexed state. They typically are esterified to glycerol or other backbone structures.
            脂肪酸在生物系统中大量存在，但很少以游离、未结合的状态存在, 它们通常会与甘油或其他骨架结构发生酯化反应

- Triacylglycerols 甘油三酯
    - A significant number of the fatty acids in plants and animals exist in the form of triacylglycerols(also called triglycerides)
    - These molecules consist of a glycerol甘油,丙三醇 esterified with three fatty acids
        - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/生物化学/images/790450_f14109a9-b5cb-44a7-825f-3d698103a96b.png)
    - Triacylglycerols are a major energy storage and the principal neutral derivatives of glycerol found in animals
        甘油三酯是主要的能量储存物质，也是动物体内发现的甘油的主要中性衍生物
    - Triacylglycerols are rich in highly reduced carbons and thus yield large amounts of energy in the oxidative reactions of metabolism
    - Triacylglycerols in animals are found primarily in the adipose tissue (body fat)
- Glycerophospholipids 甘油磷脂
    - A 1,2-diacylglycerol that has a phosphate group esterified at carbon atom 3 of the glycerol backbone is a glycerophospholipid
        - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/生物化学/images/790450_d0b227c1-6c05-4c5a-b811-8e6f13320c9a.png)
    - also known as a phosphoglyceride磷酸甘油酯 or a glycerol phosphatide甘油磷脂
    - They are essential components of cell membranes and are found in small concentrations in other parts of the cell.
    - All glycerophospholipids are members of the broader class of lipids known as phospholipids

        - The venoms 毒液 of poisonous snakes contain (among other things) a class of enzymes known as phospholipases 磷脂酶, enzymes that cause the breakdown of phospholipids

- Sphingolipids 神经鞘脂类
    - Sphingolipids represent another class of lipids found frequently in biological membranes
    - An 18-carbon amino alcohol, sphingosine 鞘氨醇, forms the backbone of these lipids
    - Typically, a fatty acid is joined to a sphingosine via an amide linkage to form a ceramide 神经酰胺
        - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/生物化学/images/790450_56e8b3c6-23aa-47ac-a98d-187dc8e089c6.png)
    - 角质层中40%~50%的皮脂由神经酰胺构成
    - Sphingomyelins (神经)鞘磷脂
        - represent a phosphorus-containing subclass of sphingolipids and are especially important in the nervous tissue of higher animals
        - A sphingomyelin is formed by the esterification of a phosphorylcholine 磷酸胆碱 or a phosphorylethanolamine 磷酸乙醇胺 to the 1-hydroxy group of a ceramide
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/生物化学/images/790450_461038cf-5cd1-46f6-8301-c277491c9595.png)
    - glycosphingolipids 鞘糖脂
        - are another class of ceramide-based lipids that are important components of muscle and nerve membranes in animals
        - They consist of a ceramide with one or more sugar residues in a glycosidic linkage at the 1-hydroxyl moiety
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/生物化学/images/790450_5f769c47-e7b0-4afa-a7da-b2bfd3f385db.png)
            它们由神经酰胺（ceramide）构成，在神经酰胺的 1 - 羟基部分（1-hydroxyl moiety）通过糖苷键（glycosidic linkage）连接了一个或多个糖残基（sugar residues）
- Waxes
    - Waxes are esters of long-chain alcohols withlong-chain fatty acids.
        蜡是长链醇与长链脂肪酸形成的酯类
    - The resulting molecule can be viewed (in analogy to the glycerolipids) as having a weakly polar head group (the ester moiety itself) and a long, nonpolar tail (the hydrocarbon chains).
        - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/生物化学/images/790450_722940dc-31c1-4fd4-d2e5-a73658e8506d.png)
        生成的分子（可类比甘油脂）可看作具有一个弱极性的头部基团（即酯基本身）和一条长的非极性尾部（即烃链）
    - Fatty acids found in waxes are usually saturated.
        蜡中含有的脂肪酸通常是饱和的
    - The alcohols found in waxes may be saturated or unsaturated and may include sterols, such as cholesterol 胆固醇
        蜡中含有的醇类则可能是饱和的，也可能是不饱和的，还可能包含固醇类物质，例如胆固醇
    - Waxes are water-insoluble due to the weakly polar nature of the ester group.
    - As a result, this class of molecules confers water-repellant character to animal skin, to the leaves of certain plants, and to bird feathers.
- Terpenes 萜tie类
    - The terpenes are a class of lipids formed from combinations of two or more molecules of 2-methyl-1,3-butadiene 丁二烯, better known as isoprene 异戊二烯 (a five-carbon unit that is abbreviated C5).
        - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/生物化学/images/790450_1fb81c1a-12a0-42c7-9e1c-158afb0eda2a.png)
        萜烯是一类脂质，由两个或多个 2 - 甲基 - 1,3 - 丁二烯 (即丁二烯) 分子组合形成，这种分子更广为人知的名称是异戊二烯 ( 一种含 5 个碳原子的结构单元，缩写为 C5)
    - A monoterpene (C10) consists of two isoprene units, a sesquiterpene倍半萜烯 (C15) consists of three isopreneunits, a diterpene二萜 (C20) has four isoprene units, and so on.
        单萜由两个异戊二烯单元构成，倍半萜烯由三个异戊二烯单元构成，二萜由四个异戊二烯单元构成，以此类推
    - Isoprene units can be linked in terpenes to form straight chain or cyclic molecules, and the usual method of linking isoprene units is head to tail
        - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/生物化学/images/790450_6b6d4aa7-ad60-440f-c2b3-d83d4cdbd700.png)
        萜烯中的异戊二烯单元可通过连接形成直链或环状分子，而异戊二烯单元常见的连接方式是 “头对尾” 连接
    - Triterpenes (三萜烯) are C30 terpenes and include squalene(角鲨烯) and lanosterol(羊毛甾醇), two of the precursors of cholesterol (胆固醇) and other steroids(类固醇)

        - beta-Carotene(胡萝卜素) is the precursor of vitamin A

- Steroids 类固醇
    - A large and important class of terpene-based lipids is the steroids.
    - This molecular family, whose members effect an amazing array of cellular functions, is based on a common structural motif of three six-membered rings and one five-membered ring all fused together.
        这类分子家族的成员能实现种类繁多的细胞功能，其结构基础是一个共同的结构母核 —— 由三个六元环和一个五元环全部稠合而成
    - Cholesterol 胆固醇
        - the most common steroid in animals and the precursor for all other animal steroids
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/生物化学/images/790450_eb71bbe5-6a39-4acd-ffbd-4f297d9531fb.png)
        - functions
            - Cholesterolis a principal component of animal cell plasma membranes, and much smaller amounts of cholesterol are found in the membranes of intracellular organelles, eg.ER.
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/生物化学/images/790450_6a327a97-7464-4195-a898-a79aacab197e.png)
            - The relatively rigid fused ring system of cholesterol and the weakly polar alcohol group at the C-3 position have important consequences for the properties of plasma membranes.
                胆固醇中相对刚性的稠环系统，以及其 C-3 位上弱极性的羟基，对质膜的特性有着重要影响
            - Cholesterol is also a component of lipoprotein 脂蛋白 complexes in the blood, and it is one of the constituents of plaques斑块 that form on arterial动脉walls in atherosclerosis 动脉硬化症.
                胆固醇也是血液中脂蛋白复合物的组成成分之一，同时它还是动脉硬化症中动脉壁上形成的斑块的组成成分之一
