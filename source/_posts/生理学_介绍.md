介绍
- Cells → Tissues → Organs → Systems
- Tissues
    - epithelial tissue
    - muscle tissue
    - nerve tissue
    - connective tissue
- About 60% of the adult human body is fluid
    - intracellular fluid
        - 2/3 of this fluid is inside the cells
    - extracellular fluid
        - 1/3 is outside the cells
        - namely the internal environment
- Homeostasis内稳态
    - maintenance of a nearly constant internal environment
    - (pH, temp., osmotic pressure, ions and etc.)
- Regulation of body functions
    - Neural regulation
        - the nervous system regulates body functions via the reflex
        - Fast and quick
        - A reflex arc includes sensors, afferent nerves, nerve centers, efferent nerves and effectors
    - Humoral regulation
        - the endocrine system secretes hormones to regulate body functions
        - Slow but durable
    - Auto-regulation
        - cells, tissues, organs regulate themselves without the participation of the nervous/endocrine system. Small scale.
- cybernetics控制论
    - negative feedback
    - positive feedback
