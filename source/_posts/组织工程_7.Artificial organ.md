- Classification of artificial organs
    - Bone/dental/joint replacements (limb, knee, hip, dental implants, etc.)
    - Soft/connective tissues (skins, cartilage, ligaments, tendons, muscles, blood vessels, etc.)
    - Sensory organs/tissues (nerves, eyes, ears, etc.)
    - Internal organs (heart, kidney, lungs, etc.)
- Clinical application
    - joint cartilage degeneration关节软骨退变, osteoarthritis骨关节炎 (OA)
        - Primary Problem
            - Damaged cartilage leads to various forms of arthritis 关节炎
            - Osteoarthrites骨关节炎 20.7 million Americans 美国有2070万患者
        - Symptoms
            - hard, bony swelling of the joints 关节硬性骨性肿胀
            - gritty feeling 关节活动时伴摩擦感
            - Immobility 关节活动受限
        - Solution
            - Total Knee Replacement (TKR)
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/组织工程/790450_c9df9274-b1d4-48ff-9498-f7e49b9eb30d.png)
            - Nearly 250,000 Americans receive knee implants each year
            - Current TKR Design
                - Three Primary Components:
                    - 1. Femoral Component
                    - 2. Tibial Component
                    - 3. Patellar Component
                    - 图
                        - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/组织工程/790450_0a8a438b-420f-497c-bd7c-81bbcf773291.png)
                - Materials - Cobalt-chromium钴铬合金, Ti-6Al-4V Titanium Alloy 钛6铝4钒合金
                - Interface - Press fit压配固定, PMMA (polymethyl methacrylate, bone cement骨水泥)
        - Results
            - Stops or greatly reduces joint pain
            - Improves the strength of the leg
            - Increases quality of life and comfort
    - Bone cement 骨水泥
        - PMMA polymethyl methacrylate 聚甲基丙烯酸甲酯
        - Key limitations
            - Non-degradable, no regeneration 不可降解、无再生功能
            - excessive stiffness 硬度过高
            - abnormal spine mechanics 导致脊柱力学异常
            - associated risk of future vertebra fracture 增加未来椎体骨折的相关风险
        - eg.Percutaneous balloon kyphoplasty (PKP) 经皮穿刺球囊扩张椎体后凸成形术
            - Treatment to compression fracture of osteoporotic vertebra 骨质疏松性椎体压缩骨折的治疗方式
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/组织工程/790450_d6a31482-e398-4f95-becc-6580ba55823c.png)

    - Dental implants
        - Drilled and placed into the jawbone.
        - Bone augmentation骨增量手术 is required if the jawbone volume is not adequate
        - Dental implant post or abutment is usually screwed into the top of the dental implant
        - 牙种植体基台通常通过螺丝固定在牙种植体顶部
        - An artificial dental crown can be made to precisely fit onto the implant post
        - 可定制人工牙冠，使其精准适配种植体基台
        - 图
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/组织工程/790450_63d053f9-435c-4404-9a14-1c5c13f4f1a9.png)
        - Osseointegration 骨整合
            - The holy grail in orthopedic/dental implant design
            - 骨科 / 牙科种植体设计中的核心目标
            - A fixture is osseointegrated if it provides a stable and apparently immobile support of prosthesis under functional loads, without pain, inflammation, or loosening.
            - 若种植体在功能负荷下，能为修复体提供稳定且无明显松动的支撑，且无疼痛、无炎症、无松动情况，则表明该种植体已实现骨整合
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/组织工程/790450_b03fcbd4-e700-442b-fbec-5a5a5d56da4c.png)
        - Two ways of implant anchorage 锚点
            - Mechanical
                - Topological features: thread螺纹, slots槽道 etc.
            - Bioactive
                - HA 羟基磷灰石 hydroxyapatite, component of bone and teeth
                - Rapid osseointegration骨整合速度快
                    - Biointegration in 4 weeks, 90% of implant-bone contact at 10 months.
                    - In contrast, Titanium only 50% implant-bone contact at 10 months

    - Total artificial heart
        - They require surgery
        - They allow mobility - a battery support patient to be “untethered” for about two to three hours.
        - They can be unwieldy笨重的 - large size and uncomfortable
        - They carry risks - infection at the two hoses that go through the skin with opening
        - They are temporary - to buy time for transplant of a donor heart

    - Artificial kidney
        - Kidneys filter some 140 litres of blood each day, leaving behind a litre or two of water and waste in the form of urine.
        - 肾脏每天约过滤 140 升血液，最终留下 1 至 2 升水分和代谢废物，以尿液形式排出体外
        - Currently dialysis machine is based on antiquated陈旧的 technology and is too bulky to be portable.
        - Miniaturization of dialysis machine into a portable device is highly desirable.
        - 将透析机微型化改造为便携式设备的需求极为迫切
        - Artificial kidney with biological components is the future.
