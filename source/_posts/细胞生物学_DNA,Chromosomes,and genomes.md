# Genomic complexity

The Definition of a Gene: The segment of DNA sequence correspond to a single protein or alternative protein variants or to a single catalytic, regulatory, or structural RNA molecule.  

TheDefinition of a Genome: The totality of the genetic information as embodied in complete DNAsequences.  

RNA molecules transcribed from the same DNA segment can often be processed in more than one 
way, so as to give rise to alternative versions of a protein.  

ncDNA noncoding DNA 非编码DNA  

intron 内含子  
exon 外显子
