## tight junctions

Tight junctions form a seal between cells and a fence between plasma membrane domains.  

### structure

![alt text](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/细胞生物学/images/19-image-9.png)

### Function 
1. Sealing function → seal adjacent相邻 cells together so that molecules cannot leak freely across the cell sheet.  
防止东西从细胞中间漏过去,比如血脑屏障  

![alt text](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/细胞生物学/images/19-image-8.png)

2. “Fence” function → help maintain the structurally polarized status of epithelia上皮 by preventing apical顶端 or basolateral基底外侧 proteins from diffusing into the wrong region.  
控制转运蛋白位置,注意下图两种转运蛋白

![alt text](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/细胞生物学/images/19-image-7.png)
## gap junctions
Gap junctions bridge gaps between adjacent cells so as to create direct channels from the cytoplasm of one to that of the other  
在相邻的细胞间形成通道可以从一个细胞质之大另一个细胞质  

![alt text](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/细胞生物学/images/19-image-10.png)

Gap junctions are present in most animal tissues  
比如connective tissues, epithelia上皮, heart muscle  

允许交换无机离子还有一些水溶性小分子  

Electrical coupling and metabolic coupling  
电耦合和代谢耦合  
电耦合指的是交换带电离子,比如电突触  
代谢耦合指的是交换代谢产物和营养物  

![alt text](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/细胞生物学/images/19-image-11.png)

# Cell adhesion molecules 细胞黏附分子

interact in 3 ways👇  
![alt text](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/细胞生物学/images/19-image-12.png)

可以分为4个families👇  
![alt text](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/细胞生物学/images/19-image-13.png)

## Cadherins 钙黏着蛋白

**homophilic adhesion**  
Ca2+ dependent  

分类👇  
![alt text](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/细胞生物学/images/19-image-14.png)  

结构👇hinge domain把它们连在一起,cadherin domain可以和另一条上的结合  
![alt text](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/细胞生物学/images/19-image-16.png)  
  

两栖动物胚胎中,cadherin丰富的会聚集在中间,不丰富的会到外面,形成不同的层👇  
![alt text](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/细胞生物学/images/19-image-17.png)


Cadherin-dependent cell-cell adhesion guides the organization of developing tissues, and epithelial-mesenchymal transitions (EMT)👇  
![alt text](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/细胞生物学/images/19-image-15.png)

## Selectins 选择素蛋白

**hectorphilic adhesion**  
Ca2+ dependent  

头上有一个lectin凝集素,可以结合细胞表面的糖👇  
![alt text](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/细胞生物学/images/19-image-18.png)

主要是在血管中介导一个暂时的细胞间adhesion,可以招募中性粒细胞去需要的组织👇  
![alt text](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/细胞生物学/images/19-image-19.png)

## Integrins 整联蛋白

**heterophilic adhesion**

Ca2+ and Mg2+ dependent  

有α β两个亚基,可以通过构型变换开关👇  
![alt text](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/细胞生物学/images/19-image-21.png)

Link the ECM to the cytoskeleton and transmit signals in both directions

![alt text](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/细胞生物学/images/19-image-31.png)

## Adhesive immunoglobulin (Ig) superfamily members 黏附免疫球蛋白超家族

**both heterophilic adhesion and homophilic adhesion**

Ca2+ independent  

Contain one or more extracellular Ig-like domains that are characteristic of antibody molecules👇
