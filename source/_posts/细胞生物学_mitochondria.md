# structure of mitochondria

outer membrane 外膜  
inner membrane 内膜  
intermembrane space 膜间隙  
matrix 基质  

![alt text](/media/细胞生物学/images/14-image.png)

## outer membrane

contains porin 孔蛋白

highly permeable  
高度可透,如ATP,NAD,CoA

## inner membrane

impermeable

contain protein with 3 type of function
1. Electron-transport chain  
电子传递链  
Carry out oxidation reactions  
2. ATP synthase  
在matrix中合成ATP
3. Transport proteins  
允许代谢物通过  
Allow the passage of metabolites

## matrix

1. the site where chemical reactions occur  
比如pyruvate丙酮酸 and fatty acid脂肪酸的氧化

2. DNA replication , RNA transcription

# ATP synthase

## structure

存在于inner membrane上,头朝着matrix

![alt text](/media/细胞生物学/images/14-image-3.png)

### F1
hydrophilic

5种9个亚基  
α3 β3 γ Ɛ δ

α和β都能结合ADP,只有α能催化ATP合成

γ forms central stalk 中间轴

γ和Ɛ form rotator 转子

a b δ form the peripheral stalk 外周柄

### F0
hydrophobic

3种亚基a b c constitute a proton channel

a b2 c8~15

c is proton channel

只有这一圈c是在转的,其他都是固定的

## mechanism

![alt text](/media/细胞生物学/images/14-image-4.png)

用H+流动drive conformational change

β 有3种conformation  
Loose,Tight,Open

rotor转子每转120°构象变化一次,合成一个ATP

# generation of H+ gradient

通过electron transport电子传递链,高能电子逐渐变成低能电子,同时把H+从matrix泵到intermembrane space

低能电子最终与O2形成H2O

![alt text](/media/细胞生物学/images/14-image-5.png)

## Electron Transport Chain 电子传递链

电子从NADH来  
NADH → NAD+ + H+ + 2e-

![alt text](/media/细胞生物学/images/14-image-7.png)

电子路径  
Complex I and II → UQ → Complex III → Cytc → Complex IV

Three H+ pumps  
Complex I, III, IV

Phosphorylation in ATP synthesis is coupled with electron transport, so the process of ATP synthesis in mitochondria is called **oxidative phosphorylation**  
ATP合成的过程伴随着电子传递因此也叫**氧化磷酸化**
