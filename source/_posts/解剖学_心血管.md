心血管
- heart
    - Location
        - In the mediastinum
            - Between the lungs
            - Mediastinum 纵隔 is a region that extends from sternum to vertebral column and from 1st rib to diaphragm
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_da1228e0-0adb-4cbe-e95f-ad6fe2b53ada.png)
        - 2/3 of the mass of the heart lies to the left of the body’s midline.
        - Visualizes as a cone lying on its side
    - 外观
        - Apex
            - formed by the tip of LV and rests on the diaphragm
            - directed anteriorly, inferiorly, and to the left
        - Base
            - opposite the apex
            - formed by the atria, mostly the LA
        - Anterior surface
            - deep to the sternum and ribs
        - Inferior/diaphragmatic surface
            - rests on the diaphragm
        - Right surface
            - faces the right lung
        - Left surface
            - faces the left lung
        - Auricle 心耳
            - resembles a dog’s ear
            - on the anterior surface of each atrium
        - Sulci
            - a series of grooves沟 on heart surface
            - contain coronary blood vessels and fat
            - external boundary for 4 chambers
                - coronary sulcus 冠状沟
                    - 分隔 atria and ventricles
                - anterior interventricular sulcus 前室间沟
                    - 分隔 RV and LV on the anterior surface
                - posterior interventricular sulcus 后室间沟
                    - 分隔 RV and LV on the posterior surface
        - 图
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_6035b9f5-3fa6-4720-fa58-697ba03ffae9.png)
    - Right atrium
        - Entry
            - Superior vena cava
            - Inferior vena cava
            - Coronary sinus 冠状窦
                - opening of deoxygenated blood from the myocardium
        - Exit
            - Tricuspid valve
        - 结构
            - Smooth posterior wall of the inner layer
            - Rough anterior wall of the inner layer due to pectinate muscles 梳状肌//左边掀起来的
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/c1e2e398-8340-465b-a40e-ed87b1a3bd1a.jpeg)
            - Pectinate muscles extend into the auricle
            - Interatrial septum (septum = a dividing wall)
            - fossa ovalis - oval depression 卵圆窝
        - 图
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_7f400135-138b-4b78-a1a4-d332c021bee7.png)
    - Right ventricle
        - Exit
            - pulmonary valve into pulmonary trunk 肺动脉干
            - pulmonary trunk divides into right and left pulmonary arteries
        - 结构
            - forms most of the anterior surface of the heart
            - inside of the RV contains **trabeculae carneae** 肉柱
            - cusps of the tricuspid valve connect to **chordae tendineae** 腱索
            - chordae tendineae connect to **papillary muscles** 乳头肌
            - RV separated from LV by interventricular septum
        - 图
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_beb3066a-4a09-4703-bada-bbd7ba724286.png)
    - Operation of tricuspid and bicuspid
        - ventricles relax → papillary muscles relax → chordae tendineae are slack → blood flows from a higher pressure in the atria to a lower pressure in the ventricles through open valves
        - ventricles contract → pressure of the blood drives the cusps upward and close the opening → papillary muscles contract → pulls on chordae tendineae → prevents the backflow of blood
        - 图
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_c22759d9-ac78-467a-f4d6-7f54116ca59f.png)
    - Left atrium
        - Entry
            - four pulmonary veins
        - Exit
            - bicuspid valve
        - forms most of the base of the heart
        - smooth inner layer as pectinate muscles only in the auricle of the LA
        - 后面看, 左右肺静脉流入左心房
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_30648596-fc6d-45f7-c59c-12440cc97722.png)
    - Left ventricle
        - Thickest chamber
        - Forms the apex of the heart
        - Contains trabeculae carneae and chordae tendineae
        - Exit
            - aortic valve into the ascending aorta
                - ascending aorta
                - arch of the aorta
                - descending aorta (thoracic aorta and abdominal aorta)
        - ligamentum arteriosum 动脉韧带 connects the pulmonary trunk and the aorta
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_6581d702-0198-4c43-a739-adbccfabe658.png)
    - Fibrous rings
        - Four dense connective tissue rings surround the valves of the heart
        - form a structural foundation for the heart valves
        - prevent overstretching of the valves as blood passes through them
        - acts as an electrical insulator between the atria and ventricles
        - 图
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_e7030eed-d5f0-4eea-ecbf-24c1e6ea43ad.png)
- conduction sysytem
    - Autorhythmic fibers - specialized cardiac muscle fibers
    - Self-excitable - act as a pacemaker, generate AP
    - Cardiac chambers contract in a coordinated manner
    - 兴奋过程
        - Cardiac excitation begins in the sinoatrial node
        - AP propagates through both atria via intercalated discs
        - Both atria contract
        - AP reaches the atrioventricular node
        - AP enters the atrioventricular bundle 房室束(希氏束)
        - AP enters both the right and left bundle branches 右束支和左束支
        - AP propagates along Purkinje fibers
        - Both ventricles contract
    - 图
        - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_dc0c6218-f374-4a92-dc8b-88f2001c88d6.png)

- Pericardium
    - Pericardium is triple-layered membrane that surrounds the heart
    - 分为
        - the fibrous pericardium 纤维性心包
            - 在浆膜性心包外面
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_51198da7-233b-4bc3-8be9-918306bd2f27.png)
        - the serous pericardium 浆膜性心包
            - 2层
                - parietal layer
                - visceral layer, AKA epicardium 心外膜
            - pericardial cavity - cavity b/t the parietal and visceral layers 心包腔
            - pericardial fluid - lubricating serous fluid 心包液
            - 图
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_617969a3-2e54-4b10-9633-4e928b0d520c.png)
- Heart Wall
    - Epicardium (external layer)
    - Myocardium (middle layer)
        - composed of cardiac muscle tissue
        - makes up approximately 95% of the heart wall
        - responsible for the pumping action
    - Endocardium (inner layer)
        - thin layer of connective tissue
        - minimizes the surface friction as blood passes through the heart

- Veins
    - 基础
        - Venous valves
            - Valve cusps point toward heart
                - allow one-way flow toward the heart
            - Prevent the backflow of blood when muscles relax
        - Mechanisms of venous return
            - Venous return - the flow of blood from the periphery back to the RA
            - Mechanisms
                - Pressure gradient
                - Skeletal muscle pump
                - e.g. contraction of gastrocnemius腓肠肌 prompts venous return
        - Artery vs. Vein
            - 单词的区别
                - Artery 叫 Branch
                - Vein 叫 Tributary - smaller veins draining into larger veins
                - Carotid 颈动脉的
                    - Common carotid A
                    - External carotid A
                    - Internal carotid A
                - Jugular 颈静脉的
                    - External jugular V
                    - Internal jugular V
            - arteries are deep, veins may be superficial or deep, deep veins go alongside arteries

        - inferior vena cava
            - 基础
                - Blood from abdominal and pelvic viscera returns to inferior vena cava. 下腔静脉的属支
                - Bood from GI tract, spleen, pancreas, and gallbladder return to hepatic portal vein. 肝门静脉系
                    - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_10ae0616-2770-4f3e-e75f-13b501a7b230.png)
                - Collects blood from the lower half of the body (below the diaphragm)
                - No valves
                - Starts at the junction of the right and left common iliac V
                - Runs on the right side of the vertebral column
                - Runs on the right of the abdominal aorta

            - Veins of the lower limbs
                - 分类
                    - Superficial veins
                        - In subcutaneous layer
                        - Anastomose with one another and with deep veins
                        - Do not go with arteries
                    - Deep veins
                        - Go with arteries
                        - Have the same names as corresponding arteries
                    - All veins of the lower limbs have valves, which are more numerous than in veins of the upper limbs.
                - Veins of thigh
                    - 1. Femoral V
                        - Accompany femoral A
                        - Extends from external iliac V at the region of the inguinal ligament 腹股沟韧带
                        - Femoral V → external iliac V → common iliac V → inferior vena cava → right atrium
                    - 2. Deep vein of thigh
                        - a tributary of femoral V 股深静脉
                    - 3. Popliteal V
                        - continuation of femoral V
                        - Femoral V goes through adductor hiatus into popliteal V.

                - Veins of legs
                    - Anterior tibial V 胫前静脉
                    - Posterior Tibial V 胫后静脉
                        - Lies posterior to the medial malleolus in the ankle and ascends 内踝后侧上行
                    - Fibular V
                        - Lies posterior to the lateral malleolus in the ankle and ascends to empty into the posterior tibial V 外踝后侧上行
                - Superficial veins
                    - Great saphenous veins
                        - Just deep to skin
                        - Begin at medial end of dorsal venous arch
                        - Pass anterior to medial malleolus of tibia 内踝前
                        - Ascends along medial side of leg and thigh
                        - **Empty into femoral vein**
                        - Have 10-20 valves, with more located in leg than thigh
                        - Varicose veins: enlarged, twisted veins
                        - Longest veins in body

                        - Deep to skin
                        - Begin at lateral aspect of dorsal venous arch
                        - Ascend along posterior aspect of leg
                        - Empty into popliteal V, posterior to knee
                        - Have 9-12 valves
