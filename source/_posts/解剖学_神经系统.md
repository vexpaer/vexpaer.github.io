神经系统
- 基础
    - 分类
        - CNS
            - brain
            - spinal cord
        - PNS
            - consists of all nervous tissue outside the CNS
            - 12 pairs of cranial N
            - 31 pairs of spinal N
    - Neuron
        - Neuron: an individual nerve cell
        - Dendrites: receiving or input portions of a neuron 树突
        - Cell body: contains the cell nucleus 胞体
        - Axon: carries the impulse away from the cell body 轴突-传导冲动
        - Myelin sheath: multilayered lipid and protein covering around some axons that insulates them and increases the speed of nerve impulse conduction 髓鞘
        - Nodes of Ranvier: gaps in the myelin sheath
    - Collections of Nervous Tissue
        - Clusters of Neuronal Cell Bodies神经元胞体聚集
            - A ganglion (plural ganglia): a cluster of neuronal cell bodies in the PNS. 神经节
            - A nucleus (plural nuclei): a cluster of neuronal cell bodies in the CNS. 神经核
        - Bundles of Axons
            - A nerve: a bundle of axons in the PNS
                - Cranial nerves connect the brain to the periphery
                - Spinal nerves connect the spinal cord to the periphery
            - A tract: a bundle of axons in the CNS 纤维束
                - Tracts interconnect neurons in the spinal cord and brain
        - White matter
            - composes of myelinated axons
            - whitish color of myelin
            - 脑-髓质
            - 脊髓-白质
        - Gray matter
            - contains neuronal cell bodies, dendrites
            - Nissl bodies impart a gray color and there is little or no myelin
            - 脑-皮质
            - 脊髓-灰质
        - 图

        - Medulla oblongata
            - 3 cm long
            - Starts at the inferior border of the pons
            - ends at the foramen magnum
            - continuous with the superior part of the spinal cord
            - Decussation of pyramids 锥体交叉
            - Pyramids 锥体束
                - formed by corticospinal tracts that control voluntary movements of the limbs and trunk
                - Superior to the junction of the medulla with the spinal cord
                - 90% of the axons in the left pyramid cross to the right side
                - 90% of the axons in the right pyramid cross to the left side
                - Each side of the brain controls voluntary movements on the opposite side of the body.

    - Cerebrospinal fluid
        - CSF
        - a clear, colorless liquid
    - CSF circulates
        - Through ventricles
        - Around the brain and spinal cord in the subarachnoid space蛛网膜下腔 (the space between the arachnoid mater and pia mater)
        - 侧脑室脉络丛产生的脑脊液经室间孔流至第三脑室，与第三脑室产生的脑脊液汇合，经中脑水管流至第四脑室，再与第四脑室脉络丛产生的脑脊液一起，经第四脑室正中孔和第四脑室外侧孔流入小脑延髓池，并迅速扩散至整个蛛网膜下隙，流向大脑背面，经Arachnoid villus蛛网膜粒渗入到上矢状窦，会流入血液循环中
        - Choroid plexus 脉络膜
            - 产生Cerebrospinal fluid

            - lateral ventricles →3rd ventricle

    - 病-A Lumbar Puncture and Epidural Anesthesia
        - A lumbar puncture 腰椎穿刺
            - Undertake a sample of cerebrospinal fluid
            - Insert into the subarachnoid space 蛛网膜下腔

        - Epidural Anesthesia 硬膜外麻醉
            - Inject anesthetic agents into the epidural space (above the dura mater)
            - Anesthetize the nerve fibers of the cauda equina
            - Commonly used during childbirth

- Cranial nerves分类
    - Cranial nerves I, II, and VIII
        - Special sensory (afferent) nerves 特殊感觉神经
        - Associated with smelling, seeing, and hearing
    - Cranial nerves III, IV, VI, XI, and XII
        - Motor (efferent) nerves
        - Contain only axons of motor neurons
    - Cranial nerves V, VII, IX, and X
        - Mixed nerves
        - Contain axons both of sensory neurons and motor neurons
    - 图

- Cranial nerves
    - I. Olfactory 嗅神经 (olfact- = to smell)
        - arises from olfactory receptors in nasal cavity which includes superior nasal concha and part of nasal septum
        - Pass through the foramina of the ethmoid bone
        - Olfactory bulb 嗅球
        - Olfactory track
        - Connects to cerebrum 连端脑
        - Function - special sense of smell
        - 病-Anosmia - Loss of the sense of smell
            - Infection
            - Swelling of nasal mucosa
            - Head injury
        - 图
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_781634b6-d35f-4bdb-f901-b23cfbe85ed5.png)

        - Visual sensory information courses to the ipsilateral同侧的 and contralateral对侧的 side of the brain at the **optic chiasm** (an X)
        - Connects to diencephalon 连间脑
        - Optic tract - pathway to occipital lobe,连接视交叉(间脑)到大脑皮层

            - motor
                - Motor to thoracic and abdominal viscera
                - Motor to muscles of soft palate, pharynx and larynx that are used in swallowing, vocalization, and coughing
