肌肉
- Muscular Tissue
    - types
        - Skeletal muscle tissue
            - move the bones
            - voluntary (under the conscious control)
            - 随意肌
        - Cardiac muscle tissue
            - only found in the heart
            - involuntary
        - Smooth muscle tissue
            - in the walls of hollow internal structures, such as blood vessels, airways, and most organs in the digestive system
            - involuntary.
    - 结构
        - Belly 肌腹
            - fleshy portion of the muscle
        - Fascicle 肌束
            - A bundle of muscle fibers
            - 由好多muscle fiber组成
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_c2ca81f9-9694-4866-a501-e1785ccbc730.png)
    - 起点与止点
        - Origin
            - the attachment to the more stationary bone
        - Insertion
            - the attachment to the more movable bone
        - 比喻上,连着门的弹簧是肌肉,连着门框的是起点,连着门的是止点
        - A rule of thumb
            - the origin is proximal and the insertion is distal
            - the insertion is usually pulled toward the origin
        - Certain muscles are also capable of reverse muscle action(RMA)
- 肌肉的形状

        - Fascicles are parallel to the long axis of the muscle

                - Anterior superior iliac spine 髂前上棘
            - insertion
                - Medial surface of body of tibia 胫骨上端内侧面

- How skeletal muscles produce movements
    - Bones serve as levers杠杆, and joints serve as fulcrums支点
    - Two different forces act on the lever
        - load (resistance)
        - effort
    - Mechanical disadvantage
        - the effort is closer to the fulcrum than the load
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_c3160a9c-c463-43f3-ca07-c33de24abbf8.png)
    - 肱二头肌
        - Long head originates from tubercle above glenoid cavity of scapula 肩胛骨盂上结节
        - Short head originates from coracoid process of scapula 肩胛骨喙突
        - Insertion on Radial tuberosity 桡骨粗隆
        - 通过这个图可以看出肱二头肌的起点止点连接
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_d27c11f6-aa67-4854-b3dd-33ac790b56a9.png)
    - Opposing pair (flexor–extensor, abductor–adductor)
        - 分为原动肌和拮抗肌
            - the prime mover contracts to cause an action
            - the antagonist stretches
        - 例
            - Flexing the forearm at the elbow
                - biceps brachii: prime mover
                - triceps brachii: antagonist
            - Extending the forearm at the elbow:
                - triceps brachii: prime mover
                - biceps brachii: antagonist
            - No movement
                - a prime mover and its antagonist contract at the same time with equal force
    - Coordination among Muscles
        - Prime mover 原动肌
        - Antagonist 拮抗肌
        - Synergist 协同肌
        - Fixator 固定肌
