腹膜&泌尿系统
- Peritoneum 腹膜
    - 基础介绍
        - Gr. Stretched round
        - 定义 - Serous membrane浆膜 that stretches around/lines the abdominal cavity
        - 作用 - Makes serous fluid
        - 组织构成 - Made of mesothelium tissue 间皮//一种上皮组织
        - parietal peritoneum 壁腹膜
            - lines the wall of the abdominal cavity
        - visceral peritoneum 脏腹膜
            - covers the organs in the abdominal cavity
        - 图
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_d39b6f18-e968-416b-fe29-49e603b09e02.png)
        - Space in between is peritoneal cavity腹膜腔, which contains lubricating fluid.
        - The GI tract pushes in the peritoneum. 消化道是向内推入腹膜中的
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_a53b5398-bacd-4123-929f-d8e36f70dd3e.png)
        - Peritoneum contains large folds大型皱襞, that bind the organs to one another and to the walls of the abdominal cavity.
        - Major peritoneal folds
            - The greater omentum 大网膜
            - The lesser omentum 小网膜
            - The mesentery 肠系膜
            - The mesocolon 结肠系膜
    - peritoneal cavity 腹膜腔
        - space between the parietal and visceral peritoneum
        - contains serous fluid
        - 病-Ascites腹水肿
            - accumulation of several liters of fluid
    - greater omentum 大网膜
        - attaches the stomach and transverse colon
        - 4 layers
        - 图
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_33dd2528-f7b2-4408-9d86-d72674f9661f.png)
    - lesser omentum 小网膜
        - attaches to the stomach, duodenum and liver
        - lesser omentum = Hepatogastric ligament + Hepatoduodenal ligament
        - 图
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_f6a078d0-bde7-4cb3-ca2e-4434c2c6048e.png)
    - mesentery 肠系膜
        - portion of peritoneum//红色部分
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_ce8a6ff1-11fd-42ed-9757-5837be137d8b.png)
        - attaches small intestine (jejunum and ileum) to posterior abdominal wall
        - double layers
        - Blood vessels and nerves go through the double layers of the mesentery to supply the abdominal organs
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_52e92479-1f6e-452b-bc59-552a6c383c1e.png)
        - 连在小肠后面//大网膜提溜起来,小肠扒拉开
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_a5f8b777-7367-4e2d-fd00-79e46c0baf24.png)
    - mesocolon 结肠系膜
        - Two separate peritoneum
            - transverse mesocolon (transverse colon )
            - sigmoid mesocolon (sigmoid colon)
        - bind the transverse colon and sigmoid colon to the posterior abdominal wall
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_28e56f6d-a407-49e2-e962-a05756c9c547.png)
    - 器官根据与腹膜相对位置的分类
        - **Intra**peritoneal viscera 腹膜内位器官
            - Stomach
            - Duodenum (1st part)
            - Jejunum and ileum
            - Cecum
            - Appendix
            - Transverse colon
            - Sigmoid colon
            - Upper rectum
            - Liver Pancreas (only the tail)
            - Spleen
        - **Meso**peritoneal viscera 腹膜间位器官
            - Ascending and descending colon
            - Middle rectum
            - Gallbladder
        - **Retro**peritoneal viscera 腹膜外位器官
            - Esophagus
            - Duodenum (2nd-4th parts)
            - Pancreas (head, neck and body)
            - Kidneys
            - Ureters
            - Urinary bladder
        - 图
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_988cfb05-6a59-4f01-ddb9-c5ff5aa053ce.png)
- Urinary System
    - 构成
        - Kidneys 肾
        - Ureters 输尿管
        - Urinary bladder 膀胱
        - Urethra 尿道
        - 图
            - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_5231b5e0-0309-46ac-9fb8-e7a7588f343e.png)
    - kidney
        - Location
            - Right kidney is slightly lower than the left because the liver occupies considerable space on the right side superior to the kidney
            - Kidneys are retroperitoneal, along side the vertebral column
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_c0727f31-a857-4888-9c44-dc6a200192c5.png)
            - locate between the levels of T12 and L2/L3
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_590d8042-fb9f-4f50-beb9-d1ae8fe4f4ff.png)
            - partially protected by ribs 11 and 12
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_880667cc-1ac5-429e-d6b4-202f015bc01f.png)
        - External Anatomy of the Kidneys
            - Near the center of the concave border is the **renal hilum**肾蒂
            - Entry & Exit for
                - Renal pelvis 肾盂
                    - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_f1b0a6b6-674a-4683-f5c0-1e67375c50fc.png)
                - Renal A
                - Renal V
                - lymphatic vessels
                - nerves
            - Suprarenal gland 肾上腺
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_dcfccaf6-078f-458c-fe6f-4db7f45b78f0.png)
        - Three layers of tissue surround each kidney
            - deep layer - the **renal capsule** 纤维囊
                - a smooth, transparent sheet of dense connective tissue
                - gives kidney its distinctive shape
            - middle layer - the **adipose capsule** 脂肪囊
                - a mass of fatty tissue
                - protects the kidney
            - superficial layer - the **renal fascia** 肾筋膜
                - a thin layer of dense connective tissue
                - anchors the kidney to the surroundings
            - 竖着劈开,最左边的是肋骨
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_ebb6a3c3-d117-46a7-c4e7-50678f2d7ef0.png)
        - Internal Anatomy of the Kidneys
            - a superficial, light red region called **renal cortex** 肾皮质
            - a deep, darker reddish-brown inner region called **renal medulla** 肾髓质
            - **renal columns** 肾柱,皮质向髓质延伸的部分，填充在肾锥体之间的空隙中
            - medulla made of 10-18 **renal pyramids** 肾锥体
            - apex (tip) of the renal pyramid called **renal papilla** 肾乳头
            - 图
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_c4af9444-9256-4781-d4f9-d0328246d4f0.png)
            - 尿液汇集
                - 希腊语calyces = cups, singular calyx
                - 1-3 papillary ducts → 1 minor calyx
                - 2-3 minor calyces → 1 major calyx
                - renal pelvis肾盂 is a single large cavity
                - The hilum expands into a cavity within the kidney called the **renal sinus**
                - Adipose tissue脂肪组织 helps stabilize the position of these structures in the renal sinus.
                - 图
                    - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_e0e87d75-b06d-4ffc-bad2-46ec36a0300a.png)
        - Functions of the Kidneys
            - Filter blood Kidneys filter 150 L of blood a day
            - If 5L blood in body that means Entire volume of blood is filtered 30 times a day
            - 病-**Peritoneal dialysis** for chronic kidney disease慢性肾脏病的**腹膜透析**
                - uses a catheter to introduce fluid into the peritoneal cavity
                - wastes in the capillaries of the peritoneum pass out of the bloodstream and into the fluid
                - fluid (with wastes) removed by catheter
                - 将透析液注入腹腔，利用腹膜毛细血管与透析液之间的渗透/扩散作用净化血液
                - 图
                    - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_45991bf8-989f-43e7-c67c-64032fc68364.png)
            - 病-Hemodialysis透析
                - filters blood through an artificial porous membrane
                - returns the dialyzed blood to the patient’s body
                - 图
                    - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_7eef535a-6678-40f9-ce87-c26e0e7c7fc7.png)
    - Ureter
        - about 25 cm long
        - retroperitoneal position
        - 3部分
            - Abdominal part 腹部
                - extends from the renal pelvis to the iliac A 髂qia动脉
            - Pelvic part 盆部
                - After crossing the pelvic brim骨盆缘, the ureter enters the pelvic cavity towards the bladder.
            - Intramural part 输尿管壁内部
                - short segment that passes through the wall of the urinary bladder
                - 1.5 cm
        - The ureter has 3 constrictions
            - Junction where the renal pelvis connects the ureter
            - Point where the ureter crosses the iliac A (either the right or left) 髂动脉
            - Junction where the ureter enters the urinary bladder
            - 这三处位置容易出现renal stone
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_dab6d69b-437e-43c4-8c0a-5e1018269de7.png)
    - Urinary Bladder
        - In the pelvic cavity
        - a muscular “bag” servers as a reservoir for the urine
        - holds up to 800 mL of urine
        - 位置
            - In males, it is directly anterior to the rectum
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_5e067763-30ca-4c85-ea90-47470ee1ec44.png)
            - In females, it is anterior to the vagina and inferior to the uterus
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_ec9b7de8-86f1-4522-ddb5-419d1fd86741.png)
        - Internal anatomy of the urinary bladder
            - Trigone
                - In the floor of the urinary bladder
                - Two corners - ureteral openings
                - Third corner - opening into urethra 尿道内口
                - Mucosa is firmly bound to the muscularis - smooth appearance
                - Sensitive to expansion
            - Muscularis consists of three layers of **smooth** muscle
                - inner longitudinal fibers
                - middle circular fibers - form an internal urethral sphincter 尿道内括约肌
                - outer longitudinal fibers
            - external urethral sphincter尿道外括约肌 - composed of **skeletal** muscle
            - 图//女性
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_03e72a55-1587-4f70-a035-4fe36f55873c.png)
    - Urethra
        - 女性
            - length of 4cm
                - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_7981e4e6-f0e0-4d86-d265-fe7009a8a0cc.png)
        - 男性
            - length of 20 cm
            - 3部分
                - prostatic urethra 前列腺部
                    - passes through the prostate
                - membranous urethra/intermediate urethra 膜部
                    - passes through the deep muscles of the perineum 会阴尿道外括约肌
                - spongy urethra 海绵体部
                    - passes through the penis
                - 图
                    - ![](https://vexpaer-blog-media.vexpaer.workers.dev/media/%E8%A7%A3%E5%89%96%E5%AD%A6/images/790450_34b106ef-d81e-44cc-ae97-4b6a4f38539a.png)
            - Urethral sphincters
                - Internal sphincter
                    - smooth-muscle involuntary sphincter at the neck of the bladder
                - External sphincter
                    - skeletal muscle voluntary sphincter surrounding the membranous urethra
        - males vs. females
            - The urethra is 5 times longer in males
            - The urethra is divided into 3 segments in males, but is only 1 short tube in females
            - The urethra is a common duct for the urinary and reproductive systems in males.
            - These two systems are separate in female.
